# Website Design and Content Rules

> Apply these rules to all pages, components, copy, and assets in this website project.

## 1. Visual Design

- **No purple gradients.** Do not use purple gradient backgrounds, overlays, buttons, or decorative sections. Choose a deliberate brand palette with simple, restrained colors.
- **No pill-shaped buttons.** Avoid fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`) CTA and navigation buttons. Prefer rectangular buttons with a modest corner radius (about 6–10px) and clear hover and focus states.
- **No emoji icons.** Use a consistent professional icon set, such as Lucide or custom SVG icons. Icons must support meaning and have accessible labels when needed.
- **No excessive scroll animations.** Avoid animating every section, parallax-heavy effects, or distracting entrance transitions. Use motion only when it helps understanding; keep it subtle and support `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **No generic AI-generated images.** Use genuine product images, real team or project photography, licensed stock photography when appropriate, or purposeful custom illustrations. Never present synthetic people, places, or work as real.
- **No "Made with AI" badge or promotional tag.** Do not add such a badge to the website UI or footer. Follow any disclosure requirements that actually apply.

## 2. Content and Copywriting

- **No vague hero text.** The main heading must clearly explain what the business offers, for whom, and/or what outcome it delivers. Use a concise supporting sentence and a relevant CTA.
- **No fake reviews.** Never invent testimonials, star ratings, customer quotes, names, or logos. Use genuine, authorized testimonials only, or omit the section.
- **No fake metrics.** Never invent customer counts, revenue, satisfaction percentages, performance claims, or "trusted by" numbers. Display metrics only when they are accurate, current, and verifiable; otherwise omit them.
- **No em dashes (`—`) in visible copy.** Prefer periods, commas, colons, or parentheses. Keep sentences clear and natural.

## 3. Required Website Essentials

- **Terms and Conditions page:** Publish an accessible, navigable Terms and Conditions page with information relevant to the actual service or business.
- **Privacy Policy page:** Publish an accessible Privacy Policy that accurately describes real data collection, use, storage, and third-party services.
- **Favicon:** Add a branded, recognizable favicon that displays correctly in browser tabs and bookmarks.
- **Custom domain:** Use the project's actual custom domain for the production website. Do not treat a temporary builder subdomain as the final public URL.

## 4. Implementation Checklist

Before launch, verify that:

- [ ] No purple gradient appears anywhere in the UI.
- [ ] Hero copy is specific and communicates the offer immediately.
- [ ] Testimonials and ratings are genuine, approved, and attributable.
- [ ] Statistics and business claims are supported by real data.
- [ ] Scroll and entrance animations are minimal and respect reduced-motion settings.
- [ ] Buttons have modest rounding rather than a pill shape.
- [ ] All icons are consistent SVG/icon-library assets, not emojis.
- [ ] Visuals are authentic, licensed, or clearly appropriate illustrations, not generic AI-generated images.
- [ ] No unnecessary "Made with AI" tag is shown.
- [ ] No em dashes appear in public-facing website copy.
- [ ] The Terms and Conditions page exists and is linked from the footer.
- [ ] The Privacy Policy page exists and is linked from the footer.
- [ ] The favicon is configured and renders properly.
- [ ] The production deployment uses the custom domain.

## 5. Priority

Treat these requirements as default project constraints. If a stakeholder requests an exception, clarify the exception explicitly instead of silently violating a rule. Do not fabricate proof, testimonials, metrics, or legal information to fill empty sections.
